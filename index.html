How to use HELLA Ventures

Your complete guide to transactions

How to Deposit

  1. Go to the Home page and click on Deposit.
  2. Select your preferred Payment Method (e.g., CBE, Telebirr).
  3. Copy the displayed Account Number and transfer the exact amount using your bank app or USSD.
  4. Enter the Amount you transferred and the Transaction Reference ID (TID) in the deposit form.
  5. Click Submit Deposit and wait for admin approval (usually within 5-10 minutes).

How to Withdraw

  1. First, link your payout account by going to Profile > Link Bank.
  2. Ensure you have a minimum balance of -- ETB.
  3. Go to the Home page and click on Withdraw.
  4. Enter the Amount you wish to withdraw and your Login Password for security.
  5. Click Request Payout. Funds will be sent to your linked bank account upon review.

About HELLA Ventures

01 — Vision
Our Heritage & Vision
HELLA Ventures represents a paradigm shift in how individuals interact with the world's most prestigious fragrance markets. Founded on the principles of exclusivity, transparency, and sustainable growth, our platform bridges the gap between everyday investors and the elite world of high-end technology perfume manufacturingamp; startup ecosystem. Our vision is to democratize venture asset ownership, allowing you to co-own exclusive funding rounds of world-renowned fragrances.
02 — Market
The Luxury Market Advantage
Historically, the venture capital market has been a walled garden, reserved for institutional investors and global conglomerates. However, the demand for premium, disruptive technology companies has seen an unprecedented, resilient upward trajectory. By investing in our curated funding rounds, you are backing tangible, high-demand assets that consistently outperform traditional financial markets. We handle the complexities of sourcing, manufacturing, marketing, and global distribution, while you earn a steady, daily return on your investment.
03 — Ecosystem
How Our Ecosystem Works
Our business model is straightforward yet highly effective. When you fund a investment portfolio through our Hub, your capital directly finances the production of exclusive startup portfolios. As these batches are produced, marketed, and sold globally at premium retail prices, the generated wholesale profits are distributed back to you daily. This ensures a consistent, passive income stream without the volatility of crypto or traditional stocks.
04 — Security
Security & Trust
Your financial security is our paramount concern. We employ state-of-the-art encryption and a rigorously audited financial structure to ensure that your principal investment is 100% safe. Every transaction, daily profit distribution, and withdrawal is processed with absolute transparency. Our dedicated support team is available 24/7 to provide personalized assistance, ensuring a seamless investment experience.
05 — Join
Join the Luxury Revolution
Step into a world of financial empowerment and exclusivity. Whether you are looking for a reliable daily income or long-term wealth accumulation, HELLA Ventures provides the perfect vehicle. Invest today, and let the timeless elegance of luxury fragrances secure your financial future.

HELLA Ventures Partner Program

Build your network · Earn multi-level commissions · Grow together

01 — Overview
What is the Partner Program?
The HELLA Ventures Partner Program is a multi-level referral system designed to reward you for growing the HELLA community. When you invite others to join and they start investing, you earn a percentage of their activity as commission — automatically and indefinitely. This is one of the most powerful ways to build passive income on the platform without any additional investment from you.
02 — Mechanism
How the Program Works
Every registered user on HELLA Ventures receives a unique referral code. Share this code with friends, family, or your network. When they register using your code and begin investing, you are credited as their direct sponsor — activating your commission earnings automatically.

03 — Commission
3-Level Commission Structure
HELLA Ventures rewards you across three levels of your referral network. You earn not just from people you directly invite, but also from the people they invite — creating a powerful compounding income effect.

04 — Benefits
Partner Benefits & Perks
Instant Commission Payout
Commission is credited to your Team Balance instantly when your referred member makes a deposit — no waiting period, no delays.
Lifetime Earnings
There is no expiry on your referral partnerships. As long as your referred members remain active investors, you continue earning commission from their activity — forever.
Milestone Rewards
Reach referral milestones (5, 10, 20, 50 active partners) and unlock bonus rewards, higher commission tiers, and exclusive investor status upgrades.
Withdrawable Commission
Your Team Balance commission can be withdrawn directly to your linked bank account at any time, just like your regular investment income.
05 — Getting Started
How to Join as a Partner
You are already a Partner by default — simply by having a HELLA Ventures account. No application or special upgrade is needed. Go to the Team section from the bottom navigation to find your personal referral code and referral link. Share it on social media, messaging apps, or directly with your contacts. Every new member who signs up using your code is automatically linked to your network.
Pro Tip: Partners who actively grow their teams and have 10+ active members consistently earn more in commission than from their own investments alone. The more active your network, the higher your total passive income.
